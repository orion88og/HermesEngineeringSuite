# Hermes Engineering Suite Blueprint Edition 3.0

Version: 3.0 (Framework Edition)

## Objective

Transform the blueprint from documentation into the governing engineering manual
for the Hermes Engineering Suite project.

## Organization

Volume I  Executive Vision
Volume II Product Specification
Volume III Architecture
Volume IV Engineering Manual
Volume V Operations
Volume VI Future Vision

## Governance Rules

1. Every feature maps to blueprint sections.
2. Every architectural change updates the blueprint.
3. Every release updates planning and operations.
4. Documentation is versioned with implementation.
5. The blueprint is the primary design authority.

## Long-Term Goal

Expand each volume into a comprehensive reference manual over the lifetime of
the project.
