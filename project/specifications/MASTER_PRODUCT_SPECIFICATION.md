# HES-BP-201 Master Product Specification

## Product Goal
Hermes Engineering Suite (HES) is the central workspace for engineering projects.

## Core Capabilities
- Project Management
- Knowledge Management
- AI Workspace
- CAD Integration
- Manufacturing
- Networking
- Business Operations
- Automation

## Functional Areas
Each capability shall expose:
- Dashboard
- Search
- Tasks
- Files
- History
- Settings
- AI Assistance
